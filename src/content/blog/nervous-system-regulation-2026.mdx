## Signs Your Nervous System Is Dysregulated

- Constantly feeling on edge or exhausted
- Poor sleep despite being tired
- Digestive issues and brain fog
- Emotional reactivity

## Daily Practices That Help

- 10-minute morning breathwork
- Somatic shaking and stretching
- Nature walks without phone
- Cold exposure (start small)
- Co-regulation with safe people
