## Why Strength Training Is Non-Negotiable After 40

After 40, women lose muscle mass faster. Strength training is the single most effective way to protect bone density, boost metabolism, balance hormones, and extend healthspan.

![Strength Training Hero](../../assets/images/strength-training-hero.png)

## Best Approach in 2026

- Lift heavy (compound movements) 3–4 times per week
- Prioritize protein (1.6–2.2g per kg body weight)
- Include progressive overload
- Recovery is more important than ever
