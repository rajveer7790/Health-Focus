## The Current State of Longevity Science

NAD+ levels decline with age. Senolytics help clear “zombie cells” that accelerate aging. In 2026, both are being studied intensely with promising early results in humans.

![Longevity Explained Hero](../../assets/images/longevity-explained-hero.png)

## What Actually Shows Promise

- NMN and NR for NAD+ support
- Fisetin and quercetin as senolytics
- Lifestyle factors (exercise, sauna, fasting) that naturally boost these pathways
