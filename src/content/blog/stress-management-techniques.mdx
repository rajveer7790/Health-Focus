Stress is unavoidable, but suffering is optional. In the modern world, our "fight or flight" response is constantly triggered—not by tigers, but by emails, deadlines, and traffic. This chronic cortisol exposure wreaks havoc on our bodies.

Mindfulness is not about silencing your thoughts. It's about observing them without judgment.

## 3 Micro-Practices for Stress Relief

You don't need to retreat to a cave to find peace. Try these tools when you feel overwhelmed.

### 1. The Box Breathing Technique
Used by Navy SEALs to stay calm under pressure.
1. Inhale for 4 seconds.
2. Hold for 4 seconds.
3. Exhale for 4 seconds.
4. Hold empty for 4 seconds.
Repeat for 2 minutes.

### 2. The 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding Method
A powerful tool for anxiety. Acknowledge:
*   **5** things you see.
*   **4** things you can feel.
*   **3** things you hear.
*   **2** things you can smell.
*   **1** thing you can taste.

### 3. Mindful Walking
Transitioning between tasks? Don't look at your phone. Feel your feet hit the ground. Notice the air on your skin. Use the transition as a reset button.

## Making Time for Stillness

We often wear "busy" as a badge of honor, but rest is productive. Taking 10 minutes a day to sit in silence can improve your focus and productivity for the other 16 hours.

Start today. Close your eyes, take a deep breath, and arrive in the present moment.
