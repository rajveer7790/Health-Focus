You can eat the cleanest diet and train like an athlete, but if you aren't sleeping well, your health will suffer. Sleep is when our body repairs tissues, synthesizes hormones, and consolidates memories.

Most of us treat sleep as an inconvenience—something we do when we're dead tired. But rethinking your sleep environment can change your life.

## The 10-3-2-1-0 Rule

A popular framework for better sleep:
*   **10 hours before bed**: No more caffeine.
*   **3 hours before bed**: No more food or alcohol.
*   **2 hours before bed**: No more work.
*   **1 hour before bed**: No more screen time (this is the hardest but most important one).
*   **0**: The number of times you hit the snooze button in the morning.

## Optimization Checklist

### 1. Light Control
Your circadian rhythm is governed by light. Get bright sunlight in your eyes within 30 minutes of waking up. In the evening, dim the lights. Use blackout curtains or an eye mask to ensure total darkness while you sleep.

### 2. Temperature
A cool room is essential for deep sleep. Aim for around 65°F (18°C). Your body temperature needs to drop to initiate sleep.

### 3. Noise
White noise machines or earplugs can be game-changers if you live in a noisy area.

## A Nighttime Routine
Create a ritual that signals to your brain it's time to wind down.
*   Read a fiction book (paper, not digital).
*   Take a warm shower or bath.
*   Do some light stretching or breathing exercises.

Prioritize your sleep tonight, and you'll win tomorrow.
