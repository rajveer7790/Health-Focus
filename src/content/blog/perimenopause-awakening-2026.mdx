## The Perimenopause Blind Spot

Most women are told perimenopause starts in their late 40s. New research in 2026 shows symptoms often begin in the mid-30s, and many women suffer for years without proper diagnosis.

## Common Signs Often Missed

- Sleep disturbances and night sweats
- Unexplained anxiety and mood swings
- Weight gain around the middle
- Brain fog and memory issues
- Irregular periods or heavier bleeding

## What Actually Helps in 2026

- Cycle syncing nutrition and exercise
- Targeted supplements (magnesium, B6, evening primrose)
- Strength training 3–4 times per week
- Stress management and nervous system regulation
- Working with a practitioner who understands perimenopause
