## No Time? No Problem.

You don’t need 2 hours a day. The most effective biohacks in 2026 are short, high-impact, and sustainable.

## 10-Minute Daily Protocol

Morning:
- 5 minutes sunlight + breathwork
- High-protein breakfast

Midday:
- 10-minute walk after lunch
- 2-minute cold face plunge

Evening:
- Magnesium + wind-down routine
- No screens 1 hour before bed

These small habits compound dramatically over time.
